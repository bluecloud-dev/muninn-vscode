A calmer writing space

Bring the tools into one place, and let the available space decide which ones stay visible.

Keep your attention on the document

Headings and formatting stay close to the words. A wider pane reveals more tools; a narrow pane keeps every action within reach.

  • Heading choices open beside the toolbar.
  • More contains only what does not fit.
  • The document keeps its reading space.
